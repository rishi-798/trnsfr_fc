# Pilot review — 2026-10-17

Reviewed 20 of the 50 player-seasons across all seven sample categories: **17 ok, 3 issue**. The other 30 review rows remain blank. Only `review_status` and `review_notes` were changed in `pilot_sample.csv`; no model inputs, labels, training flags, or pipeline code were changed.

## Findings

Three labels disagree with Transfermarkt's dated valuation history:

| Player / season | CSV value_end | Transfermarkt value at cutoff | Valuation date | Correct age at cutoff |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| George Hemmings / 2025–26 | Missing | €800,000 | 2026-06-03 | 27 days |
| Will Lankshear / 2024–25 | Missing | €3,000,000 | 2025-05-27 | 34 days |
| Alex Palmer / 2024–25 | €900,000, 760 days old | €1,000,000 | 2025-05-30 | 31 days |

All three are currently excluded from training. Their verified valuation ages would satisfy the existing 180-day label rule. The raw valuation file has no records for Hemmings or Lankshear, and Palmer's latest raw record is 2023-06-01. This locates the discrepancy in source coverage relative to today's Transfermarkt history, rather than the June 30 join. The review cannot determine whether the missing historical points were added to Transfermarkt after the dataset snapshot.

For all 20 reviewed rows, Premier League appearances, minutes, goals, assists, and per-club minute splits match the current Transfermarkt match records. The exported sample omits club identity; club checks therefore reconstruct the pipeline's most-PL-minutes club from raw appearances and compare its identity and minute split with Transfermarkt. Final league positions and Champions League stages were outside this review.

Missing history has several valid explanations:

- Championship: Palmer (West Brom), O'Brien (Huddersfield), Iroegbunam (QPR), Kelly and Cook (Bournemouth), Cork (Burnley), Omobamidele (Norwich), and Muniz (Middlesbrough). The dataset's competition list excludes GB2, so missing previous-season totals are expected.
- Andrey Santos: Vasco da Gama in Brazil. Transfermarkt records six Série A appearances and 424 minutes in April–June 2023; the raw BRA1 fixtures start in March 2025. This is a historical coverage gap, not Championship football.
- Brandon Austin: Transfermarkt lists no played matches in 2023/24 (18 in-squad and 23 not-in-squad entries). Missing prior stats therefore do not imply an uncovered league.
- Moore and Monga: no valuation by the applicable cutoff is correct. Their first dated values are 2024-10-01 and 2025-09-05 respectively. Their previous-season appearances were youth football. `no_valuation` describes the absence of an earlier valuation, but does not itself explain why senior-league stats are absent.

Rondón and O'Brien really do have stale labels at their cutoffs: the latest eligible values are dated 2022-11-03, 239 days old. Their existing training exclusions are correct. Rondón's 2023-07-04 update must not be pulled backward across June 30.

The movers also follow the intended rule. Fofana totals Chelsea plus Leicester and uses Chelsea as his main club; Reguilón totals Brentford plus Manchester United and uses Brentford; Rashford totals United plus Villa but uses United because he played more PL minutes there. Zaniolo's previous 1,246 minutes and six goals combine Roma and Galatasaray; `prev_league=IT1` identifies his majority league, not the sole source of those totals. This matches the current implementation but should be documented when interpreting the feature.

## Recommendation

Address valuation coverage before moving to the model, beginning with a targeted refresh or explicitly sourced correction policy for the three flagged rows. Consider retaining club ID/name in the review export and distinguishing uncovered leagues, unavailable historical coverage, and no appearances in missing-stat explanations. The sample deliberately emphasizes problematic records, so 3/20 is not an estimate of the full dataset's error rate.

## Method and evidence

The CSV was opened in VS Code. Each selected player's public Transfermarkt statistics and valuation-history pages were retrieved. Their tables and graphs are dynamically populated: exact match records and dated graph points were read from Transfermarkt's public component endpoints, rather than estimating points visually. Review judgments were then entered row by row.

`season` is the starting year. The value check selects the latest EUR valuation on or before June 30 of the following year, not the nearest point after that date. Season statistics include only GB1 appearances with participation state `played`. Previous-season context is checked separately; cup and youth matches are not substituted for senior domestic-league history. `ok` means these requested checks agree with the existing scope and missing-data rules; it does not mean a missing or stale row is eligible for training.
